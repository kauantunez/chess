Piece moves

  • ♖ Rook: straight lines, any distance.
  • ♗ Bishop: diagonals, any distance, one colour only.
  • ♕ Queen: straight lines and diagonals, any distance.
  • ♔ King: one square any direction.
  • ♘ Knight: an L (two squares, then one sideways). Jumps over pieces.
  • ♙ Pawn: one forward (two from its starting row), captures one diagonally forward. Cannot move forward onto an occupied square.

No jumping (the knight is the exception). Land on an enemy to capture it. Your own pieces block you.
