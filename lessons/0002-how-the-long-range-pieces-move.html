How the rook, bishop, queen and king move

Your win: for each of these four pieces, you can point to every square it can move to.

Written from general knowledge, not a verified source (the FIDE and Lichess sites are still unreachable). Re-check against FIDE Laws of Chess, Article 3.

The rules in four lines

  • Rook ♖: any number of squares in a straight line, forward, back, left or right.
  • Bishop ♗: any number of squares diagonally. It stays on its starting colour forever.
  • Queen ♕: any number of squares in a straight line or diagonally (rook plus bishop).
  • King ♔: one square in any direction.

None of these can jump over another piece. Landing on a square with an enemy piece captures it; your own pieces block you. The drills below use an empty board, so nothing blocks you yet.

Practice: tap every square

Tap each square the piece can reach, then press Check. Gold squares are ones you missed, red ones are wrong.
