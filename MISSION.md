# Mission: Chess

## Why
Play casual games of chess with friends and family, enjoy them, and not get crushed.

## Success looks like
- Set up the board and move every piece legally without looking anything up
- Recognise check, checkmate and stalemate, and finish a game properly
- Play a full casual game against a friend or family member
- Avoid giving away pieces for free (basic "is my piece safe?" habit)

## Constraints
- About 10 minutes per session: very short lessons, practised often
- Total beginner: starts from how the pieces move

## Out of scope
- Rated or tournament play
- Deep opening theory and advanced endgames
